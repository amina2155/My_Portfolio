Form-aware Poetic Generation for Bangla

Research Paper — Accepted at BLP Workshop, IJCNLP-AACL 2025 · Jun 2025 – Sep 2025

  • Developed a form-aware Bangla poetry generation framework for low-resource settings by introducing variable structural control tokens for rhyme, meter, word count, and line boundaries.
  • Fine-tuned pretrained Bangla LLMs, including TigerLLM, TituLLM, and BanglaT5, on general Bangla text augmented with structural controls to improve adherence to poetic form while maintaining semantic coherence.

Re-Think: Interactive Two-Stage Self-Regulated Reasoning for LLMs

Ongoing Research Work · May 2025 – Present

  • Developed an interactive reflective prompting framework that externalizes intermediate reasoning through editable metacognitive questions, allowing users to modify, delete, or add reasoning steps before final answer generation.
  • Evaluated the framework across multiple reasoning benchmarks and through a 60-participant user study, investigating reasoning accuracy, user trust, confidence, engagement, and interaction with LLM reasoning.

Dynamic and Periodic Proof of Evolutionary Model for Blockchain in IoT

Undergraduate Thesis — Published in PLOS ONE · Aug 2023 – Sep 2025

  • Designed PoPI (Proof of Periodic Inference), an ML-based consensus mechanism for resource-constrained IoT environments that periodically uses supervised learning to evaluate dynamic node conditions and select block producers.
  • Developed and evaluated a stochastic blockchain simulation across networks of up to 1,000 nodes, demonstrating improvements in latency, throughput, and computational overhead compared with existing consensus mechanisms.

CricShot10k: Cricket Shot Detection and Dataset Development

Research Project — Published in IEEE Access · Aug 2024 – Jan 2026

  • Developed CricShot10k, a large-scale cricket shot dataset containing 10,086 video clips across 15 shot categories, collected from 536 match-highlight videos spanning diverse formats, tournaments, and player groups.
  • Proposed CricShotNet, combining player/bat localization, cropping and segmentation with EfficientNetV2-S and GRU-based temporal modeling, achieving 89% top-1, 97% top-2, and 99% top-3 accuracy.
